RelaxRing+ – recorded voices
============================

The voice files in this folder (*.mp3) were generated with Piper
(https://github.com/OHF-Voice/piper1-gpl) from the voice models published at
https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices. Each file holds the short phase
words of one language (inhale, hold, exhale, ...); voices.json lists where each
word starts and how long it is.

the other gender was not available under such a license either.
