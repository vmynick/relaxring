RelaxRing+ – nature sounds
==========================

The background sounds in this folder (*.mp3) are 50-second loops cut from field
recordings published on Freesound (https://freesound.org) under the Creative
Commons 0 license (public domain dedication). CC0 does not require attribution;
the sources are listed here with thanks to the recordists.

Each loop was taken from a steady part of the recording, its end crossfaded into
its start over 3 seconds so that it repeats without a click, and its level
normalized. White and brown noise are synthesized by the app.
